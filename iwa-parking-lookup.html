Golden rules — every Med quote

Quote reposition by default at any red-rated destination Jul–Sep. Remove only once overnight parking is confirmed in writing.

Diarise booking windows in Stack. Spain 14–15 days out · Naples/Salerno 15d · Faro & Brindisi 10d · Florence 15d–24hrs. Chase the operator the morning it opens.

Slot discipline. Greece FLS (29 May–4 Oct): FPL vs slot mismatch over ±15 min = auto suspension. Nice: ±10 min arrival. Client schedule change → re-coordinate the slot first.

Hidden charges. Mykonos / Corfu / Kos: EUR 2,000 per pax sector GA terminal fee (+VAT non-EU operators). Check operator quotes include it.

Warn clients early that parking confirmation often lands only days before the flight at hotspots — set the expectation at quote stage.

Heat & fuel. Nice restricts APU · Linate has denied early APU · Malaga & Madrid report fuel delays (airline priority). Plan ground power and buffer.

Non-EU operator to Portugal? EORI number may be needed even for private flights or tech stops.
